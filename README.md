# Polished English — Project Overview

## Mission
A conversational English tutor for proficient but non-native speakers who work in professional environments where small grammatical errors undermine credibility. The app targets people who learned English later in life and already communicate well but persistently make subtle mistakes — the kind that mark them as non-native and can affect workplace perception, tips, promotions, and client trust.

## Target Audience
- **Who:** Immigrants and second-language English speakers already in the workforce at a high level
- **What level:** Intermediate to advanced (B2–C1 CEFR). NOT beginner English; this is *polishing* English
- **Where:** Three real-life workplace settings: hospitality, office/corporate, retail/customer service
- **Core problem:** "I know English but I still say 'more better'" — habitual errors that persist without correction

## The Three Pillars
1. **Common Mistakes Library** — Categorized errors with correct alternatives, explanations, and context
2. **Workplace Scenarios** — Real-life role-play exercises for hospitality, office, and retail settings
3. **Best Practices & Exercises** — Research-backed techniques for building error-free conversational habits
