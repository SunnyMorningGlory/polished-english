How to Express Opinions Respectfully

Use these phrases to share your views while being considerate of others' perspectives.

Agreeing

I completely agree with that.
That's a great point — I couldn't agree more.
You make a good case for that.

Disagreeing Respectfully

I see it differently, but I understand your perspective.
That's an interesting take. I hadn't considered it from that angle.
I can see why you'd say that, but I think...
I respect your opinion, though I have a different view.
I understand where you're coming from, but...

Expressing Uncertainty

I'm not sure I agree, but I can see the reasoning.
I don't have a strong opinion on this, but...
I'd need to think about that more before forming an opinion.

Building on Others' Points

Building on what you said, I'd also add that...
To piggyback on that point...
That reminds me of something similar I read about...

Asking for Others' Opinions

What's your take on this?
How do you see this playing out?
I'd be curious to hear your perspective on this.
